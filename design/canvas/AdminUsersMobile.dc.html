Rules the app always keeps

  • Nobody changes their own roles.
  • At least one active approver and one active admin always remain.
  • Approvers never see drafts and never approve a request they prepared or executed.
  • Every change is in the audit log, and approvers see it under Role changes on their Approvals page.

Nobody is ever deleted: a deactivated person can't sign in, and everything they prepared or signed stays on the records.
