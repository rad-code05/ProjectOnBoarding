What does "Returned" mean, and what do I do next?
Read the help page Statuses & who signs

Returned means the approver sent the request back to IT instead of confirming it. Their comment and the flagged sections are shown at the top of the request.

  1. Open the request and read the comment.
  2. Fix the flagged sections — they are open again.
  3. Press Review & sign again; you can add a note for the approver.
