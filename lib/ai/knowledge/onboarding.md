title: Onboarding requests
audience: it
summary: Creating an onboarding, the form's sections, office and nationality, applications and equipment, execution and section 9.
---
**Start one:** *Requests → New request → Onboarding*. A ticket number like UAM-2026-000152 is given straight away; the draft saves itself as you type.

**The form (form v4.2):**
1. Request details (type, priority) — set by the app.
2. Employee: name, work email, job title, department, manager, **Office** (required — picking it fills the work country), for the US office also **State** and **City**, **Nationality** (optional, up to two), effective date.
3. Employment event and notes.
4–5. **Application access** — the catalog in two columns; for each app choose the permission and Grant or Enroll. *Add other application* for something not in the catalog.
6. **Equipment** — Issue a laptop, phone, etc. (asset tag optional).
7. **Physical / logical access** — badges, doors, VPN.
8. Offboarding details (offboarding only).
9. **IT execution checklist** — Yes / No / Not needed per item; ticked by a person, never by the assistant.
10–11. Signatures (filled when people sign).

**Execution:** *Start execution* moves the request to *In execution*; do the work, tick section 9, then *Review & sign*.

**With the assistant:** say who joins ("Onboard Meera Nair, Dubai, Engineering, starts Monday, Slack and GitHub"); it asks follow-up questions, shows a *Needs your Confirm* card and creates the draft with every value as a suggestion. On the form: the black banner counts the suggestions (*Accept all*, *Next suggestion*, *Reject all*); each dashed field or row has its own Accept / Reject. Review & sign warns about suggestions not reviewed — they are not part of the request until accepted.
