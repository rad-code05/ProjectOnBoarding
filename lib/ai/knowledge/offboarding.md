---
title: Offboarding and the SLA
audience: all
summary: Who is leaving, the access-removal deadline (SLA), removal methods, equipment return, mailbox, met or missed.
---
**Start one (IT):** *New request → Offboarding → Who is leaving?* — pick the person. Their access, equipment and office come from what closed requests gave them (the access inventory). One open offboarding per person.

**The SLA** — the deadline for removing access, counted from the **cut-off** (date and time, company timezone):
- *Same day* — by 23:59 of the cut-off day.
- *Within 24h* / *Within 48h* — the cut-off time plus 24 or 48 hours.

**Met or missed:** met when the last access removal was ticked before the deadline (server time); otherwise missed, and IT gives a reason. Equipment returns are not part of the SLA.

**During execution:** each application is removed with a method (Delete, Suspend or Disable), equipment is marked Returned or Not returned (with a reason), and the mailbox is recorded as converted to shared or "no mailbox to convert". A device still *Return pending* is a warning, not a block.

The *Offboarding SLA* report lists deadlines, met / missed and upcoming ones.
