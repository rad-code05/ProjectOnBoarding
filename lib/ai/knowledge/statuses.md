---
title: Statuses & who acts
audience: all
summary: What Draft, In execution, Awaiting confirmation, Returned, Closed and Cancelled mean, and who acts next.
---
Every request (onboarding, offboarding, access change) moves through these statuses:

- **Draft** — IT (Raju) is filling in the request. Only IT sees drafts; approvers never do.
- **In execution** — IT pressed *Start execution* and is doing the work: granting or removing access, issuing or collecting equipment, ticking section 9.
- **Awaiting confirmation** — IT pressed *Review & sign* and signed. The request now waits for the approver (Moises, or Celine as backup). It shows on their *Approvals* page.
- **Returned** — the approver sent it back to IT instead of confirming, with a comment and the sections that need attention flagged. Those sections are open again; IT fixes them and presses *Review & sign* again (optionally with a note for the approver).
- **Closed** — the approver pressed *Confirm & sign*. The record is final, the PDF record is made, and the request appears under *Records*.
- **Cancelled** — IT cancelled it with a reason. Cancelled requests are left out of the reports.

Rules that never change: nobody approves their own request, all dates and times are set by the server (never typed or backdated), and every change is recorded in the audit log.
