---
title: Access changes
audience: all
summary: Changing someone's access — keep, change or remove what they hold, add new access, and what the record shows.
---
**Start one (IT):** *New request → Access change → Who is changing?* — pick the person. The request lists everything they hold today (from the access inventory).

For each thing they hold choose **Keep**, **Change** (for example a new permission, shown as "was → now") or **Remove**; add new applications, equipment or access like on an onboarding. Office, location and nationality can change too ("Was: …" shows the old value).

Section 9 is pre-filled: *Yes* where something changed, *No changes made* elsewhere.

When it closes, the record and the PDF show **What changed** — before and after — and the access inventory is updated.
