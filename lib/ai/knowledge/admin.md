*Admin* (admins only):
- **Applications** — the catalog shown in sections 4–5: categories, each app's permissions (roles), several roles in one row where allowed. Retire instead of delete.
- **Equipment & access types** — what can be issued in section 6 and the physical / logical access in section 7.
- **Offices** — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Geneva, US…; an office can ask for a state, then a city (the US). Cities per state can be added here or straight from the City field on a request.
- **Settings** — the company timezone (used by PDFs, ticket numbers, reports and SLA deadlines).
- **AI settings** — assistant on / off, the model in use, adding models (name, Anthropic model ID, prices), the monthly budget and questions per person per hour.

Everything is retired rather than deleted, so old records still read correctly, and every change is recorded in the audit log.
