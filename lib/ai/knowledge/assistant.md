---
title: The Laine assistant
audience: all
summary: What the assistant can and can't do, where its answers come from, chats, limits and the budget.
---
The assistant answers questions about **this app and its data only** — reports, people and access (for the IT side), and how the app works. It has no web search and no outside knowledge.

- **Every answer names its source** — which report and filters, or which help page — with a link to open it.
- **It never signs, confirms, returns, closes, cancels, ticks section 9, changes roles or settings, or deletes anything.** Those are not tools it has.
- **Section 9 notes:** tell it what was done ("Slack invite sent, laptop enrolled") and it drafts the *Implementation notes* as a suggestion. You accept it once execution has started; it is added after any notes you already wrote. The checklist ticks stay yours.
- **Preparing an onboarding** (admins and requesters): tell it who joins; it always goes through the whole checklist first (work email, job title, department, office, manager, start date, applications and roles, equipment, access, nationality — say "not needed" for what doesn't apply), shows every value and waits for your **Confirm**, then creates the draft. Each value arrives on the form as a **suggestion** (dashed outline) you accept or reject — one by one or *Accept all*. Nothing is sent to Slack, GitHub or any tool, and asset tags, section 9 and signing stay yours. Every accepted value shows **AI** in the audit log. To add or change something on a draft that already exists, give its ticket ("add the email meera@laine.ai to UAM-2026-000404"): it adds suggestions there, never a second draft. The robot button shows how many suggestions wait on the request you have open.
- **Approvers** (Moises, Celine) get report questions only.
- It uses **your own access**: it can't see anything you can't see in the app.
- **Chats** are private to you and deleted 60 days after the last message.
- **Limits:** a number of questions per person per hour and a monthly budget for the whole company (set by the admin in *Admin → AI settings*). When a limit is reached it says until when.
- It can be wrong — open the source to check.
