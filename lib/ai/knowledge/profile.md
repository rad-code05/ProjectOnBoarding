audience: all
summary: Your timezone, signature and initials, password and MFA, and what the assistant remembers.
---
*My profile* (your initials, top right):
- **Timezone** — the times on *your* screens. PDF records, ticket numbers, reports and SLA deadlines always use the company timezone, so everyone sees the same record.
- **Signature & initials** — upload a PNG, draw your signature, or use initials; choose which one you sign with. Old versions are kept, so past records still show the signature used at the time.
- **Password & MFA** — change your password (you confirm who you are first), set up the authenticator app on a new phone, or make new backup codes.
- **What the assistant remembers** — the name it uses for you, favourite reports, frequent questions and request types (never your chat text). *Clear my memory* and *Delete my chats* remove them; chats are deleted anyway 60 days after the last message.

Your name, email and roles are managed by the admin.
