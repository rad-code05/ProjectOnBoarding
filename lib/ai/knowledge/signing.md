---
title: Signing, returning and confirming
audience: all
summary: Review & sign (IT), Confirm & sign or Return (approver), what a signature means, and where to set yours up.
---
**Review & sign (IT).** When the work is done, IT presses *Review & sign*. The sheet shows the checks (required fields, section 9), a summary of the request and the signature preview. IT ticks "I confirm" and signs. The request moves to *Awaiting confirmation*.

**Confirm & sign or Return (approver).** The approver opens the request from *Approvals*:
- *Confirm, sign & close* — they check the summary, tick "I have reviewed this record" and sign. The request closes and the PDF record is made.
- *Return to Raju* — they flag one or more sections and write a comment. The request goes back to IT as *Returned*.

**What a signature is.** An internal acknowledgment that the process was done — not a legal e-signature. It is applied only by the signer, after they confirm, with the server's date and time. The app records a fingerprint of exactly what was signed; if the request changed after IT signed, the approver can't confirm the old version.

**Your signature.** Set it up on *My profile*: upload a PNG, draw it, or use initials (typed or as a PNG). Only you can apply it; nobody else — not even an admin — sees your signature image except on requests you signed.
